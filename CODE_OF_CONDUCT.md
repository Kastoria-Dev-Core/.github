# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Kastoria Dev Core to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity. Members, contributors and maintainers pledge to keep participation in our projects and community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are just starting out.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a university setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the organization maintainers. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
