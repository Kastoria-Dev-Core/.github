---

We are a small group of students, mostly from the Computer Science department of the University of Western Macedonia in Kastoria, who build things together in our free time. Some projects are useful for the city and the university, some are just for fun. Everything is open source.

## Projects

| Project | What it is | Stack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [astiko-ktel-kastorias-app](https://github.com/Kastoria-Dev-Core/astiko-ktel-kastorias-app) | Android app with the lines, stops and timetables of the Kastoria city buses | Kotlin |
| [astiko-ktel-announcements](https://github.com/Kastoria-Dev-Core/astiko-ktel-announcements) | Daily cron that mirrors the official bus announcements into a JSON feed for the app | JavaScript, Vercel |

## Want to join?

- Students from any department or year are welcome, no prior experience needed.
- Pick an issue labeled `good first issue` in any repo, or open one with your own idea.
- Read the [contributing guide](https://github.com/Kastoria-Dev-Core/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) before your first pull request.

## Στα ελληνικά

Είμαστε μια ομάδα φοιτητών στην Καστοριά που φτιάχνουμε open source projects, άλλα χρήσιμα για την πόλη και το πανεπιστήμιο και άλλα απλά για πλάκα. Αν θες να συμμετέχεις, διάλεξε ένα issue ή άνοιξε ένα με τη δική σου ιδέα.
