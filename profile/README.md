# Kastoria Dev Core

Welcome to the Kastoria Dev Core organization! We are a team of students in Kastoria, Greece, who build open source projects together.

## About Us

Kastoria Dev Core is a group organized by students in Kastoria, aiming to build useful software for the city and the university, and to learn by working on real projects as a team. Some of our projects solve everyday problems, like getting around with the city buses, and some are just for fun. Everything we make is open source. Our team mainly consists of students from the Department of Informatics of the University of Western Macedonia, but we welcome anyone interested in contributing in any way they can.

## Projects

- [astiko-ktel-kastorias-app](https://github.com/Kastoria-Dev-Core/astiko-ktel-kastorias-app): Android app for the Kastoria city buses, with lines, stops, timetables and announcements.
- [astiko-ktel-announcements](https://github.com/Kastoria-Dev-Core/astiko-ktel-announcements): Daily cron that mirrors the official bus announcements into a JSON feed for the app.

## Get Involved

Participation in our projects is voluntary and open to students of any department or year, with or without prior experience. To get started, pick an open issue in any of our repositories, or open a new one with your own idea.

For more information, feel free to open an issue or a discussion in any of our repositories.
